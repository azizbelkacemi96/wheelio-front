  <files>src/features/customers/mutations.ts, src/features/customers/mutations.test.tsx</files>
  <behavior>
    mutations.test.tsx (MSW + fresh QueryClient):
    - individual (no drivers): calls POST /customers once, no driver POST, resolves with the created customer
    - company + zero drivers: POST /customers once, NO driver POST attempted, resolves success
    - company + N drivers, all 201: POST /customers then N sequential POST /customers/{id}/drivers in order, resolves with all drivers succeeded
    - company + N drivers where the k-th driver POST fails (MSW 400): the customer is created (not rolled back), drivers before k succeeded, the result reports the created customer + the succeeded count + the failed row indices (does NOT throw away the customer id)
    - a retry of only the failed rows against the already-created customer id succeeds and completes attachment
    - on full success the ['customers'] cache is invalidated
  </behavior>
  <action>Create `src/features/customers/mutations.ts` exposing `useCreateCustomerMutation()` (TanStack Query `useMutation`) that orchestrates the create-then-attach sequence over the 03-01 api.ts functions. Step 1: `createCustomer(body)` → capture the returned customer (its id). Step 2 (company branch only, when there are driver rows): iterate the driver rows SEQUENTIALLY with `for … await` (NOT Promise.all) calling `createDriver(customer.id, driverBody)`, so a mid-list failure has a clear "created customer + first N drivers" state and the error points at a specific row index. Partial-failure contract (research): once step 1 returns 201 the customer is persisted — do NOT roll it back (no transactional endpoint exists). Model the mutation result as a discriminated outcome: full success (customer + all driver results) vs partial (customer + succeeded indices + failed indices/errors). Provide a way to retry only the failed rows against the existing customer id (e.g. the returned partial result carries the customer id and the failed driver bodies so the form can re-invoke attachment without recreating the customer). On FULL success, `queryClient.invalidateQueries({ queryKey: ["customers"] })`. Do not navigate here — navigation is the form's concern. Individual customers never enter step 2. Keep PII out of logs.</action>
